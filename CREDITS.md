## Generated assets

Most particle textures and sound effects are generated by the scripts in
`Tools/AssetGenerators/` (for example `particle_textures.py` and
`sound_effects.py`). They are part of this project and are covered by its MIT
license. Re-run the scripts to tweak or regenerate them.
