# Code of Conduct

Kamikaze Hero follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, and assume good intent. Harassment,
insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing other
people's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or
any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainer, Ali Reza Dehdar
([@Dehdar](https://github.com/Dehdar)), privately through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or
block anyone who does not follow this code of conduct.
